## 🧭 The 8 shifts, all within reach

| # | The shift | The Architect | The Developer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | When you join | Shapes the spec | Builds from it |
| 2 | How you handle the ask | Questions the ask | Clarifies the ticket |
| 3 | How you communicate | Talks business and tech, by audience | Talks tech, with the team |
| 4 | What you diagram | Maps the system | Draws the feature |
| 5 | How you choose tools | Picks across the platform and beyond | Picks within the task |
| 6 | How far ahead you think | Thinks in years | Thinks in sprints |
| 7 | What you deliver | Owns the decision and writes it down | Builds the solution and ships the code |
| 8 | What your day looks like | Decides and communicates all day | Builds all day |

---

## ✅ You don't need a new title to start. Pick one today!

- **Question the ticket before you build.** Don't build the ask as written. Ask why it exists and what it's missing, before you write a line.
- **Draw before you build.** Sketch the ticket's flow or data model before you start.
- **Pick the right tool, not the familiar one.** Your default is code. On your next requirement, ask what clicks could do first, then pick what's right for the job, not what's comfortable.
- **Write down the why.** Made a decision, picked one approach over another? Write a few lines: the context, the options, the why. Architects call it an ADR. That's your first one.
- **Get in the room.** At the next design or solution discussion, ask to sit in and make it useful: take notes or draft the decision record.
- **Own a small design.** Ask to own the design of one feature end to end, not just the build.

## 📚 Official Salesforce resources for architects

The best places to keep learning how to *think like an architect*, straight from Salesforce:

- **Architecture Center:** the home for all of it: Well-Architected, diagrams, decision guides, and the fundamentals.
  👉 https://architect.salesforce.com/
- **Think Like an Architect:** a livestream and blog series that teaches the *What / How / Why* approach.
  👉 https://www.salesforce.com/blog/think-like-an-architect/
- **Well-Architected Framework:** the standard for solutions that are Trusted, Easy, and Adaptable (a great tool for reviewing your own decisions).
  👉 https://architect.salesforce.com/well-architected
- **Salesforce Diagramming Framework:** how to draw clear architecture diagrams (with Lucidchart and the Salesforce shape library).
  👉 https://architect.salesforce.com/diagrams
- **Decision Guides:** guidance on the trade-offs behind common architecture decisions (integration, data, async, and more).
  👉 https://architect.salesforce.com/decision-guides/
- **Salesforce Architects (YouTube):** short videos and deep dives from the Architecture Program.
  👉 https://www.youtube.com/@SalesforceArchitects
