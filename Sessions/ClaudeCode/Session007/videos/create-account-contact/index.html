Step 1 of 12
Start on the Salesforce Home screen.
Step 2 of 12
Open the Accounts tab to see your accounts.
Step 3 of 12
Click into an account to open its detail page.
Step 4 of 12
Select the Related tab to see connected records.
Step 5 of 12
Find the Contacts related list, and click New.
Step 6 of 12
A blank New Contact form opens.
Step 7 of 12
Enter the contact's first and last name.
Step 8 of 12
Add their title, mobile number, and email.
Step 9 of 12
Review the completed form before saving.
Step 10 of 12
Click Save to create the new contact.
Step 11 of 12
The new contact now appears in the Contacts related list.
Step 12 of 12
Open the contact to confirm everything was saved.
